### Rulings (governor, 2026-10-30)

1. **A juggler is a pair of parts.** Juggler j is parts 2j and 2j + 1 (`jugglerOf(part) = ⌊part / 2⌋`), as the stage has paired them since the lanes ruling 4. No new stored state: the parts, the hash, `Parts` and `Part` are unchanged. A pass is a flight with `jugglerOf(from) ≠ jugglerOf(to)` (`isPass`). An odd last part is a one-handed juggler, as today.
2. **Notation, form 4: passing siteswap.** `<3p 3|3p 3>`: one vanilla sequence per juggler between `|`, a digit followed by `p` a pass to the other juggler, `p<n>` (1-based) naming the target, required when there are three or more jugglers (the digit after `p` is the target iff it names an existing juggler other than the thrower, otherwise it is the next throw, so `<3p3|3p3>` is `<3p 3|3p 3>`; a bare `p` is then a `bad-target`, as a bare `x` is among three parts); spaces optional; the angle brackets optional in the pattern box and absent in the link. Inside this form `p` is the pass mark and height 25 cannot be written, as `x` is not 33 in vanilla; outside it `p` stays the letter for 25. Every juggler's sequence is laid out as `vanillaPair` lays one out (spacing 2, phases 0 and 1, unit 1; an odd-length sequence read twice), at parts 2j and 2j + 1, so all jugglers throw their phase-0 hand together. A throw of height h at siteswap position i of juggler j lands in part `2t + (i + h) mod 2`, where t is the target juggler (j itself when the throw is not a pass): odd passes land in the target's other-phase hand, even passes in the same-phase hand, the same parity rule as vanilla's crosses (`MATH.md` §9.6 corollary). Up to `MAX_JUGGLERS` (3) jugglers; more is `too-many-jugglers`. `MATH.md` §10 states this; P1 opens Juggling Lab's notation page to confirm that its `<…|…>` form starts every juggler in the right hand (so our phase-0 hand is the right hand and an odd pass is right to left, "straight" for two jugglers facing) and records the URL there.
3. **`Event` gains `to: PartIndex`**, the part that catches the flight (for a catch event, `to === part`; for a faulted group's stand-in flight, `to === part`). The sound needs the landing part to hear a pass, and the pictures already compute it (`catchPart`), which becomes a read of `e.to`. A governor ruling on the frozen types, recorded here.
4. **Sound.** `Mapping` gains `pass: { velocity: number; sample: Sample }`: a pass throw plays `sample` in place of the sound it would have played when `sample` is not null, and gets `velocity` on top of the cross accent (both clamped into `MAX_VELOCITY`). Default `{ velocity: 20, sample: null }` in every preset: no pattern that plays today has a pass, so nothing changes its sound. Hash key `q: [velocity, sample]`, absent in old links, repaired from the default. With three or more parts the Sound tab shows one Kit select per juggler ("Juggler 1 kit", …), each setting the kit of that juggler's parts, in place of the one select; and a "Passes" section with the accent and the pass sound. With one or two parts the panel is as it is.
5. **The passing card.** `<3p 3|3p 3>`, six balls, cycle 2, a juggling card in the first-minute set (`first: true`), id `passing`, mapping height-to-drum grown to four voices with juggler 1 on the TR-808 at pans −0.8 and −0.4 and juggler 2 on the LM-2 at 0.4 and 0.8 (a `PASSING_CARD` tunable). Its link is `#v2.` with `{ n: 'height-drum', k: [...] }`, as a sourcebook card's is.
6. **Short link.** `#3p-3|3p-3` (`-` for the space, no brackets, `|` between jugglers), with `~PRESET` and `@BPM` as the other short forms, meaning the preset on default kits; `|` is what makes it form 4. `route.ts` records the grammar; browsers that percent-encode `|` are covered by the existing `decodeURIComponent`.
7. **The editor.** At 1:1, a passing set (`isPassingSet`) shows as one input holding `<3p 3|3p 3>`, as a vanilla pair shows as one input holding `531`; a draft containing `|` reads as form 4; a draft with neither `|` nor `/` over a passing set reads as vanilla over two parts (back to one juggler). The Rhythm tab shows the parts as lanes, as it does for any four parts. The digit pad gains a `p` key beside `x`, shown only while the input is a passing one.
8. **The stage.** Unchanged in geometry (figures side by side, `figureStride` apart, a pass the same parabola across). With three or more parts each figure gets a caption under its hands ("Juggler 1", …) and the canvas label names the jugglers, as it does. Three jugglers must fit at 390 px wide with balls no smaller than `minRadiusPx` (screenshot). The world-coordinate motion model for a later 3D stage is not built now.
