## What was wrong (headless at 390×844 and 1280×800 on anomatom.com, 2026-10-31)

1. The card screen was two apps: six juggling cards, then nineteen sourcebook rhythms played by a juggler whose rests are dropped balls. The juggler is not what makes those rhythms interesting.
2. "More rhythms" on a wide screen grew the page to 3,229 px: the card column grows the document instead of scrolling inside itself (the phone already scrolls in `.fm-scroll`). A longer juggling list would do the same.
3. Between "pick a card" and "edit everything" there was nothing: while a card plays the only controls are Pause and Make your own; kit, mapping and tempo live in the sheet, reached only through Make your own, which opens on Pattern.
4. Once you leave the card screen there is no way back to it short of loading the bare URL (a card writes the hash, so a reload skips it too).

## Rulings (governor, 2026-10-31)

1. **The sourcebook cards go.** Every `kind: 'sourcebook'` row and every layered card leaves `PRESETS`, and their data leaves the code (`ROWS`, `LAYERS`, `sourcebook()`, `layered()`, `voiced()`, `hitGrid` if nothing else reads it, `URLS`, their `copy.presets` entries, "More rhythms"/"Fewer rhythms" and `cardsShown`/`moreCount`). `Preset.kind`, `Preset.bpm` and `Preset.first` go with them if nothing else needs them. About's "where the cards come from" list (one source line per sourcebook card) goes; `docs/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md` and the rest of About stay. `docs/POLYRHYTHMS.md` stays as a document. Tests that borrowed a sourcebook preset as a fixture build the same parts inline. An old `#v2.` link to one of those rhythms still opens, since a link carries its whole state.
2. **The classics, grouped by ball count.** The card screen is headed groups: "3 balls", "4 balls", "5 balls", "Passing" (6 balls and up, two or three jugglers). A card's group is its ball count, the passing ones excepted. The 3:2 card (`2 2x 2 / 2x 2`, 4 balls) sits in "4 balls". Draft list, every name and every pattern confirmed against a source opened in the stream and recorded in the PR (memory: verify citations before they land); a pattern whose name cannot be sourced keeps a descriptive name ("High, middle, low") rather than an invented one:
   - 3 balls: 3 (cascade), 51 (shower), 441, 531, 423, 52512, 55500 (flash), and the box once sync lands (ruling 5).
   - 4 balls: 4 (fountain), 53 (half shower), 534, 7531, 3:2 two hands, and (4,4) once sync lands.
   - 5 balls: 5 (cascade), 744, 645, 97531.
   - Passing: `<3p 3|3p 3>` (two-count), `<3p|3p>` (ultimates), `<3p 3 3|3p 3 3>` (three-count), `<3p 3 3 3|3p 3 3 3>` (four-count), checked against Juggling Lab's passing notation.
   Every card keeps playing height to drum on the reader's tempo (no card sets tempo now). The cascades are one drum on every beat by design; mixed heights are where the music is.
3. **The card column scrolls inside itself on every screen size.** The first-minute region is bounded by the viewport (the bar with Make your own and Play stays put) and `.fm-scroll` scrolls; the document never grows past the viewport on the card screen at 390×844, 844×390, 1280×800. Group headings scroll with the cards.
4. **A "now playing" strip and a way back.** On the card screen, directly above the bar, one row (wrapping to two below 360 px) of three controls that act on whatever the store holds:
   - **Kit**: a select of `KIT_IDS`, dispatching the existing `MappingEdit` `{ kind: 'kit' }` through `applyEdit`. When the parts' kits differ (the passing cards), it shows a disabled "Mixed" option selected; choosing a kit sets every part, as the Sound tab's one select does.
   - **Sound**: a select of `PRESET_NAMES` by `copy.presetNames`, dispatching `{ kind: 'preset' }` (which keeps kits). A custom mapping shows a disabled "Custom" option selected.
   - **Tempo**: a − / + stepper around the bpm readout (`setBpm`, step `TEMPO_STEP` in `tunables.ts`, 5), clamped to `BPM_RANGE`.
   - A text button "All sound settings" after them opens the sheet on the Sound tab (`sheetTab: 'sound'`), leaving the card screen as Make your own does.
   - **Back to the patterns.** The sheet gets a "Patterns" control (in the head row, before the summary, an icon plus `aria-label` on a phone and a word on a wide screen) that shows the card screen again over the current state without stopping playback; the card that matches the playing state lights as today. The card screen still opens by itself only on a bare URL.
   - The strip reads the store on `draw` and never keeps its own copy; edits go through the same paths the Sound tab and the tempo knob use, so the hash, the player and the Sound tab stay in step. Space keeps working after a select change (wave 1 ruling 3).
5. **Sync notation, form 5.** `(4,2x)(2x,4)`: each pair is the left and right hand throwing on the same beat, every height even, `x` crossing to the other hand; `*` after the sequence repeats it mirrored (`(4,2x)*` is the box). Two parts at spacing 2, both at phase 0, unit 1 (both hands throw together every two pulses, as Juggling Lab and the siteswap literature count it); a throw of height h from hand k at beat b lands at b + h in hand k, or in the other hand when crossed. Odd heights are `bad-sync` (a new `ParseError` kind); a lone `(4,4)` is legal (4-ball sync fountain). `MATH.md` §11 states the layout and the ball count (sum of heights / (2 × pairs)) and records the source opened for the notation. `isSyncSet(parts)`, `syncText(parts)` (`(4,2x)(2x,4)`, the `*` form never written back), `textOf` returns `syncText` for either part of a sync set. In the editor a sync set shows as one input holding its text, as a vanilla pair does; a draft starting with `(` reads as form 5. The digit pad does not gain `( , )` keys this wave (follow-up): sync arrives by card or a hardware keyboard. A sync set's link is `#v2.` (no short form this wave). Sync cards: the box `(4,2x)(2x,4)` in "3 balls" and `(4,4)` in "4 balls", plus any other sync classic whose name is sourced (Juggling Lab's sync examples, e.g. `(6x,4)*`, if named there).
