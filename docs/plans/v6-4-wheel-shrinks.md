# v6.4 · the phone wheel shrinks instead of closing

Status: built 2026-11-04 (W7, PR 120; one Opus review, one fix round). Two bends as built: a tap that clicks a row of the compact wheel is a row tap (ruling 4), and only a press and lift with no click grows it (ruling 3), DECK.growGraceMs after the lift so a slow tap's click still lands on its row; focus on a chip when the wheel shrinks goes to the wheel's tab stop, since the chips hide. Ruling 5 revised by the governor after the review: the deck under the compact wheel does not close it.

## 0. Decided with Nico, 2026-11-04 (smoke of #117)

- On a phone, after a turn settles, the wheel closes and is gone. Nico would rather it get smaller but still read as a wheel: about three rows, the chosen one in the band in the middle, the top of the top row and the bottom of the bottom row cut off a little or faded, as the seven-row drum's outer rows are.
- Nico: "go, you got it" — the rest are the governor's calls (section 1), marked as such.

## 1. Rulings (build governor, 2026-11-04)

1. **Compact, not closed.** On a phone upright, where the return timer (`DECK.returnMs` after the settle, or after the last finger lifts: #116) closed the wheel, the wheel goes compact instead. Compact: the drum over the foot of the stage, where the open-at-rest wheel sits now (`wheelPlace`), `DECK.compactRows` rows tall (new tunable, 3), the chosen row in the band in the middle, the outer rows fading toward their outer edges as the seven-row drum's do. The deck bar and sound row show under it as when the wheel is closed. The stage keeps the compact wheel's room clear (`--wheel-pad-*`), so the juggler draws above it, larger than with the seven-row wheel. Governor's call: the outer rows are faded and the drum's edge cuts them by about a quarter row; the builder may tune the cut and fade by screenshot so it reads as a wheel and the middle row is plainly the chosen one.
2. **No chips when compact.** The Balls/Jugglers chips hide while compact; the juggler gets their room. (Governor's call.)
3. **Touch grows it.** A turn on the compact wheel takes the sheet as any turn does today (spinning: `sheetWheelPlace`, seven rows or more, chips shown), settles, and goes compact again `DECK.returnMs` later. A touch on the compact list that does not turn it (a press and lift, no scroll) grows it back to the open-at-rest wheel (seven rows, chips), which stays until the next turn's return makes it compact. (Governor's call.)
4. **A row tap plays and goes compact.** A tap on a card row, from the open or the compact wheel, plays it (as today) and leaves the wheel compact rather than closing it. A tap on Make your own still closes the wheel and opens the Pattern tab, as today. (Governor's call.)
5. **What still closes it.** Tapping the pattern name (it toggles: compact or open → closed), Escape, Details, Make your own, and a pointerdown outside the picker and the pattern name close it fully, as today. A closed wheel opens from the pattern name at seven rows with chips, as today. A first visit still opens on the seven-row wheel, playing nothing. *Revised 2026-11-04 (governor, after the review of PR 120):* a tap on the deck bar or the sound row under the compact wheel (Play/Pause, ‹ ›, mute, the volume slider, Listen for) does not close it: it stays compact and the band follows the pattern. Details still closes it, the pattern name still toggles it closed, and Escape and a tap elsewhere (the stage, the top bar) still close it. The open seven-row wheel keeps today's tap-outside behaviour.
6. **Only upright phones.** A phone on its side and a short wide screen (the column placement) keep their behaviour: they never had the return timer. Docked is untouched.
7. **Accessibility.** Compact counts as open: `aria-expanded="true"` on the pattern name, the rows keep their roving tab stop. Focus: where the return closed the wheel with focus inside and moved it to the pattern name, compact leaves focus where it is.
8. **The SPEC.** Add a dated v6.4 paragraph after the v6.2 one in `docs/SPEC.md` saying: on a phone upright the wheel shrinks to three rows after it settles instead of closing; it closes from the pattern name, Escape, Details, Make your own or a tap outside. Update the `DECK.returnMs` doc comment in `src/tunables.ts` likewise.
