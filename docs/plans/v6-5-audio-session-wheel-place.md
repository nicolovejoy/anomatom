### A · the audio session (#123)

1. **Park, don't close.** `DrumSound` gains `park()`: it marks the context parked, suspends it (`ctx.suspend()`), and sets `navigator.audioSession.type` to `'auto'` where the browser has one (iOS Safari). `resume()` un-parks: it already sets `'playback'` (v6.3, the ringer switch) and resumes a suspended context, so it needs only to clear the mark. A park keeps the context, its kits and pads and its clock; nothing reloads. Park with no context made yet (or no Web Audio) does nothing and makes none. (Governor's call: `audioSession.type` is WebKit's own API, the one iOS already honours for this page, and `'playback'` is the category that makes a page a now-playing app; `'auto'` hands that back. Suspend alone may leave a paused card; the two together are the lightest thing that can work.)
2. **When it parks.** *Provisional, reversible (coordinator):* a playing anomatom keeps sounding with the page hidden; the session is let go only when it is not playing. In the player (the one place with timers):
   - Pause or Stop, page visible: park `AUDIO_RELEASE_MS` later (new tunable, 2000), so the glide (at most `LOOKAHEAD_SECONDS`) and the drums' tails finish. A Play before then cancels it.
   - Page hidden while paused or stopped, or paused while hidden (an iOS interruption in the background pauses, v6.3 ruling 1): park at once. A hidden page's timers may never fire.
   - Page hidden while playing: nothing (provisional). `AUDIO_PLAYS_HIDDEN` (new tunable, `true`) holds the branch: `false` makes hiding while playing a press of Pause followed by an immediate park. Both branches are unit tested, so the flip is one line.
   - Page shown again: nothing. Never a resume without a gesture (v6.3 ruling 1; iOS refuses one anyway). Play, Enter or a card tap resumes through the existing gesture path.
   The player gains `setHidden(hidden: boolean)`; `src/main.ts` wires `visibilitychange` (`document.visibilityState === 'hidden'`), `pagehide` (hidden) and `pageshow` (`document.hidden`) to it. Mute is not a pause: a muted, playing anomatom keeps the session.
3. **The anchor needs nothing new.** A suspended context's clock stops but keeps its value; it is the same clock, stood still. The player is paused or stopped whenever it parks, and `resume()` and `play()` already re-derive everything from `sound.now()` at the gesture (`d = now + START_DELAY_SECONDS − frozen`; a fresh segment at `now + START_DELAY_SECONDS`). A park mid-glide (hidden within 0.1 s of Pause) leaves hits handed over and unsounded in the context; `resume()`'s glide branch (`now < frozen`) already plays them on and shifts nothing. The only place a clock is replaced stays `renew` and `checkClock`'s shift: if WebKit resumes a parked context silently, that path rebuilds and re-anchors it, as for any gesture. No second re-anchoring path is written.
4. **A park is not an interruption.** The `statechange` listener ignores a context that is parked or whose park's `suspend()` is still settling (a Play inside that window must not be paused by the late `'suspended'` event). `park()` clears `mark`, so a `renew` due from a gesture just before returns false and reports nothing; the player also clears its stall check when it parks. A context that stops while *not* parked is still an interruption (the v6.3 spec suspends from the page while playing and must still see Pause). `close()` clears the parked state.
5. **No `navigator.mediaSession`.** Left out. The Media Session API describes and adds controls to a now-playing entry that WebKit makes from the page's own audio; `playbackState` is a hint for a browser that cannot see the state, and nothing in it removes the entry. Setting metadata or handlers would make anomatom more of a player, not less. If the device check fails after rulings 1 and 2, the next lever is closing the context on park (rebuilt at the next gesture, re-anchored through the `checkClock` shift), not mediaSession; that is a follow-up, not this wave. (Governor's call; unverifiable headless.)
6. **The Sound status row is unchanged.** A park is invisible by design: the transport already says Play, the samples stay loaded (the context and its buffers are kept), so `ready` stays true. No new strings; `src/ui/copy.ts` is not touched.
7. **What the fakes carry, and what only a phone can.** Unit, `src/sound` at 100% lines: the fake context gains `suspend` (a deferred promise, so the settling window is testable); park suspends and sets the session to `'auto'`; resume after park sets `'playback'` and resumes; a `statechange` while parked or while the park settles is not heard, one after resume is; `renew` after park returns false and reports nothing; park with no context makes none; close clears the park. Player (`src/app`, 100%): pause and stop park `AUDIO_RELEASE_MS` later, not before; play or resume before then cancels; hidden while paused or stopped parks at once; paused while hidden parks at once; hidden while playing parks nothing with `AUDIO_PLAYS_HIDDEN`, and pauses and parks without it; shown does nothing; a clock stood still across a park resumes `START_DELAY_SECONDS` ahead of the gesture. Device only: whether iOS drops the media card, and whether a playing anomatom in the background keeps time (a hidden page's timers are throttled and the look-ahead is 0.1 s, so it may stutter; if it does, that is the argument for the other branch and goes to Nico).
8. **The SPEC.** A dated v6.5 paragraph after rule 15 in §5 (Behaviour rules): paused or stopped, anomatom lets go of the audio session (at once when the page is hidden, two seconds after Pause otherwise), so iOS shows no media controls for it; playing, it keeps sounding in the background (provisional); only a gesture brings sound back. Doc comments on the new tunables and on `park` likewise.

### B · the wheel above the strip (#124)

9. **Above the strip.** On a phone held upright, the compact wheel *and* the open seven-row wheel at rest sit directly above the ladder strip, which stays at the foot of the stage, under the wheel, with the deck bar and the sound row under it. At 390×844 that puts the compact wheel at about 534 to 614 and the strip at 614 to 734, the juggler 44 to 534 (as tall as today); the wheel at rest at about 350 to 614 with its chips, the juggler 306 tall (as today). (Governor's call: moving the rest wheel too keeps the strip still between rest and compact; the juggler loses nothing.) The order of places, in `wheelPlace` and `compactWheelPlace`: above the strip when at least `DECK.minFigurePx` of stage stays above the wheel and the strip together; else over the stage's foot, under the strip, as today (a short phone, an iPhone SE); else down the stage's side, as today. The spinning wheel that takes the sheet (`sheetWheelPlace`), a phone on its side, a short wide screen (the column) and docked are unchanged. The ladder's height is measured from `.region-ladder` and passed in.
10. **The juggler's room.** Above the strip the stage keeps no room at its foot (`--wheel-pad-bottom` 0); the juggler's region keeps the wheel's height clear under it instead: `WheelPlace` gains `padFigure`, set as `--wheel-pad-figure` and applied as `margin-bottom` on `.region-juggler` while the wheel is open (a margin, not a padding: the canvas is `position: absolute; inset: 0`, so padding would not shrink it). The over-the-foot and side places keep `--wheel-pad-bottom` and `--wheel-pad-right` as today, with `padFigure` 0; closed, all three are 0. The rule goes in `deck.css` (or `style.css` beside the `--wheel-pad-*` rule if the cascade needs it; A does not touch `style.css`).
11. **Linger: `DECK.returnMs` 1500 → 3000, one tunable.** On a phone upright the controls' return *is* the shrink: the deck bar comes back under the compact wheel at the same moment. A separate shrink delay would need a new phase between the sheet and compact (seven rows over the stage with the deck under it) that nobody asked for. So the settled wheel keeps the sheet 3 s, the deck bar hidden for those 3 s; #116's rule holds (3 s after the last finger lifts). `settleMs` and `growGraceMs` unchanged. A phone on its side never had the timer. (Governor's call, reversible: if the hidden Play bar for 3 s bothers Nico, the separate phase is the follow-up.)
12. **The e2e that change.** Every bound on the return delay reads `DECK.returnMs` from `src/tunables` where the spec can import it, not a literal:
    - `e2e/phone-wheel.spec.ts`: the four `≥ 1500 − 20` bounds and the `> 1500 + 700` one; the holds meant to outlast the delay (2000, 2500, 3000 ms waits in the #114 and #116 tests, the drag-on-compact test) go past 3 s; `expectCompact`'s `timeout: 4000` goes past 3 s with margin; `expectCompact` gains the place: the wheel's bottom at or above the strip's top, the strip's bottom at or above the deck bar's top, the strip in the viewport; the first test's juggler-above-compact check stays and the rest wheel's bottom now sits on the strip's top; header comments say 3 s.
    - `e2e/phone-chip-turning.spec.ts`: the two "past the would-be close" waits of 2500 ms go past 3.16 s; its header says 3 s.
    - `e2e/filters.spec.ts`: `timeout: 3000` for compact (line 322) and `4000` (line 396) go past 3 s with margin; the "past the return delay" wait of 2500 ms (line 385) goes past 3.16 s.
    - `e2e/deck.spec.ts`: `timeout: 3000` for compact (line 398). The upright "pattern name opens" checks hold as written (over the stage, juggler clear) and gain: the wheel above the strip.
    - `e2e/sync.spec.ts` and `deck.spec.ts` line 201 assert compact after a row tap, which is at once: unchanged.
13. **The SPEC.** A dated v6.5 paragraph after the v6.4 one in §4.1: on a phone upright the wheel, open or small, sits above the ladder strip, the strip under it and the deck under that; it shrinks 3 s after a turn settles. The v6.4 paragraph's "1.5 s" and "over the foot of the stage" are replaced by a pointer to it, as v6.2's paragraph does.
