## 1. Rulings (build governor, 2026-11-06)

Probed before ruling, on main at 13c0732:

- `uprightRows()` (mount-deck.ts) returns `DECK.compactRows` (3) whenever the phase is `compact`, sheet open or not; grown, `grownRows` with `DECK.sheetFigurePx` (120) as the juggler's floor when `#app.sheet-open`, else `DECK.minFigurePx`. The juggler's CSS floor with the sheet open is `--deck-sheet-figure` (style.css). So with the sheet open the card at rest is the chips and three rows, and the sheet gets what the band, 120 px of juggler, the strip and the sound row leave: about 50 px of panel at 375×667, about 224 at 390×844 (issue #133's measures).
- The band row's grid row is shared with the side column (`'wheel side'`): its height is the taller of the card and the column (Play 48 px, then mute and the slider). A card shorter than the column saves nothing below the column's height.
- The list's keydown pages by `phase === 'compact' ? DECK.wheelRows : shownRows` (mount-deck.ts, the `rowAfterKey` call): from compact always seven, whatever the grown card then shows (five at 375×667, three with the sound's status row showing). `rowAfterKey` (deck-model.ts) takes the page as an argument already.
- `touching` is set by a `touchstart` on the list and recounted only by a `touchend`/`touchcancel` heard on the list, from `e.touches` filtered by `list.contains(t.target)`. `applyFilter` calls `buildList`, which replaces the list's `innerHTML`: a finger held on a row is then on a detached node, its lift fires there and never bubbles to the list (issue #121).
- The coordinator's lean for #121 (a document-level `touchend` that recounts `touching` from live touches inside the list) has a hole the probe shows: after the rebuild the held finger's `target` is the detached row, so `list.contains(target)` is false for it. A document listener would then hear the *chip* finger's lift, recount zero list fingers while finger A is still down, and arm the return under it: issue #116 back. Touches must be known by `identifier`, not by target.
- The rows have no rules between them (`.deck-row` has no border), so "give the empty slots the list's row rule" has nothing to copy. The blank slots are the list's `::before` and `::after` (deck.css), `(rows − 1) / 2` rows tall: one row on the compact card, three on the grown one. On the first preset at rest (Cascade) the top slot is bare `--surface`, dimmed by the compact gradient; on Make your own the bottom one is.
- e2e: the synthetic `touch()` helper in phone-wheel.spec.ts dispatches a plain `Event` with a `touches` list of `{ target }` objects, no `identifier`, no `changedTouches`. `stopPlaying` (e2e/fixtures.ts) reads `#play`'s text once (`textContent()`, no wait) and then clicks `#stop` (mount-knobs.ts, in the sheet, `disabled` until something plays).

### A · the phone wheel's loose ends

Scope: the upright phone (`upright()` in mount-deck.ts) unless a ruling says otherwise. On its side, short wide and docked are unchanged pixel for pixel, except ruling 5's end mark, which every drum with ends shows.

1. **#133: with the sheet open the card rests on its band row** (governor's call). Upright with `#app.sheet-open`, the compact card is the chips and one row, the band row: a new `DECK.sheetRows` (1), read by `uprightRows()` in place of `DECK.compactRows` when the sheet is open. The chips stay (v6.6 ruling 3 is Nico's: chips always). The one row is the band row, not dimmed (the compact gradient's thirds do not apply to a single row; deck.css), and it shows the name as v6.6 ruling 9 has it (notation, name, meta or "next cycle"). Row r still sits in the band at scroll r × rowPx: one row shown has no blank slots, so nothing else moves. Closing the sheet gives the card its three rows back at once, the band on the same row.
   - **A turn with the sheet open** grows the card in place as v6.6 ruling 7 says (to `grownRows` with the sheet's floor, the sheet's flex giving the room), and `DECK.returnMs` after the settle or the last finger's lift it goes back to its one row, not three. A press and lift grows it after the grace, as on the three-row card. A tap on the one row plays it. Escape and a tap outside shrink a grown card to one row; the sheet stays open.
   - **The juggler's floor.** First measure, then lower only if needed. The gate, at 375×667 with the sheet open on the Pattern tab: the hand input and Swap (issue 82's row) wholly inside the panel's visible box without scrolling the panel, and no page scroll. If the one-row card does not meet it (the side column may set the band's height: section 1's probe), `DECK.sheetFigurePx` comes down in 8 px steps, never below 80, until it does; the PR states the value and the panel heights at 375×667 and 390×844 before and after. If 80 still does not meet the gate, stop at 80 and report it: the next lever is the side column's layout, which is Nico's design and not this wave's.
   - Reversible: `DECK.sheetRows = DECK.compactRows` restores v6.6 exactly. Rejected: dropping the chips with the sheet open (it reverses a ruling of Nico's for about 44 px), and lowering only `sheetFigurePx` (it shrinks the juggler first, where the card's two dimmed rows are worth less to a reader who is editing).
2. **#134: PageUp and PageDown page by the rows the card will show** (governor's call). A pure helper in deck-model.ts, `keyPage(phase, shown, grown)`: `grown` when the phase is `compact` (a key's turn grows the card first), else `shown`. `grown` is `grownRows` as `uprightRows()` measures it for the grown card (factored so it can be asked from compact without growing: the chrome, the strip, the sound row and the sheet are all measurable at rest), so a short phone pages five, a shorter one or one with the sound's status row three, and 390×844 seven as today. With the sheet open (ruling 1) `grown` is the sheet's grown count. Off upright the call is unchanged (`shownRows`). Unit tests: one per case, 7, 5 and 3 grown rows from compact, and one from `spinning` returning `shown`. `deck-model.ts` stays at 100% lines.
3. **#121: list touches are known by identifier** (governor's call; the coordinator's lean, amended). The mount keeps a set of the touch identifiers that started on the list: a list `touchstart` adds each of `e.changedTouches` (targets are attached at that moment); a `touchend` or `touchcancel` heard on `document` (capture, passive) keeps only the identifiers still in `e.touches`. `touching` is "the set is not empty". The work the list's touchend does today (the band home, the held return, the lapse, the settle) runs on the step from not empty to empty, wherever the lift was heard; a lift of a finger that never touched the list (a chip finger with no list finger down) changes nothing, as now. A `touchstart` anywhere also drops identifiers not in its `e.touches`, so a lift the page never heard (a touch lost to a system gesture) clears on the next touch, as today. The set's update is a pure helper in deck-model.ts (`heldTouches(held, live)`, the intersection), unit tested: one held, one lifted, a stranger's lift, all lifted. Rejected: recounting by `list.contains(target)` from `document` (section 1: it drops a held finger on a detached row), and listening on the picker (the chip and the list are both inside it; it hears the same detached-target problem).
4. **The e2e for #121, two touch points.** The issue's check, at 390×844: a finger held on a row, a second finger taps a chip (the list rebuilds), the first finger lifts, nothing else: the card goes compact `DECK.returnMs` after that lift (timed in the page with `stampPhases`), and is still grown just before. Plus the inverse that guards #116: finger A held on a row, finger B taps a chip and lifts; the card stays grown while A is held, past `PAST_RETURN`. Chromium drives real touch points by CDP (`Input.dispatchTouchEvent`, two `touchPoints` with ids, so hit-testing, the chip's click and the detached target are the browser's own); WebKit, which has no CDP, by the synthetic helper, which gains `identifier` on each touch and a `changedTouches` list, the lift dispatched on the row element held from before the rebuild (detached, as the real one is). The existing touch tests (116, 119, 118, the slow tap) move to the identified helper and keep their assertions.
5. **The empty slots at the ends show a faint end mark** (governor's call). On every drum with ends (upright and on its side; never docked, which has no blank slots), the blank slot next to the first row and the one next to the last draw a short centred line: 24 px wide, 1 px, `--rule`, vertically centred in the slot's row nearest the end, a CSS background on the list's `::before` (at its foot) and `::after` (at its top). No text, nothing announced (a pseudo-element; the list's label and roving tab stop are unchanged), nothing to tap. The slots beyond it stay bare surface. Not a wrap-around and not a row: the wheel has ends (v6.2), and the mark says so. On the compact card the dim gradient covers it as it covers the slot. With the sheet open (ruling 1) the card shows no slot, so no mark. Reversible: one CSS rule. Rejected: leaving the slot bare (the live site's blank row reads as a missing card) and a word such as "start" (a new string where a line does).
6. **The SPEC.** A dated v6.7 sentence after the v6.6 paragraph in §4.1: with the sheet open the card shows the chips and its band row, growing as before when turned; past either end of the wheel a short line marks the end. No other SPEC change; #134 and #121 are fixes to what v6.6 already says.

### B · the done-means flake (#135)

8. **Wait on the state, not a snapshot** (governor's call). `stopPlaying` (e2e/fixtures.ts) reads `#play`'s text once and clicks `#stop`; under four workers the read can come before the transport says it is playing, and `#stop` is `disabled` until it does. The builder first reproduces with a trace (`npx playwright test e2e/done-means.spec.ts --workers=4 --repeat-each=10 --project=edge`, headless, `trace: 'on'` for the failing run) and reads it: which of `#play`'s text, `#stop`'s `disabled`, and `data-events-scheduled` lag. Then `stopPlaying` waits on what it needs: `#stop` enabled (`toBeEnabled`), and Pause pressed only when `#play` reads Pause after that wait. The 3:2 test waits on the playing state before its assertions (it does, by `data-events-scheduled`), so its own body changes only if the trace shows otherwise.
9. **An app race, if the trace shows one.** If `#stop` stays disabled or hidden while `data-events-scheduled` is past 0 (the page is playing and says it is not), that is the app's bug: fix it in its file (likely `src/app/mount-knobs.ts`, where `#stop` is), with a unit test on whatever pure part decides `#stop`'s state, and say so in the PR. No fixed waits (`waitForTimeout`) added anywhere.
10. **The proof.** The whole file 10× with four workers, headless, on Edge and on WebKit: `--repeat-each=10 --workers=4`, every run green, the counts in the PR. Then the whole e2e once, since `stopPlaying` is shared across the specs.
